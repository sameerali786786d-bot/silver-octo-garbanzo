Admission Process

Simple and transparent process for the academic session 2026-27

1️⃣

Step 1: Enquiry

Visit the school or fill the online enquiry form. Collect the prospectus and admission form.

2️⃣

Step 2: Registration

Submit the filled form with required documents and registration fee.

3️⃣

Step 3: Interaction

Student interaction / assessment (as applicable) and parent meeting with the Principal.

4️⃣

Step 4: Confirmation

On selection, complete the admission formalities and fee payment within the given timeline.
