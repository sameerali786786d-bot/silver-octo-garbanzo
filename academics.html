Curriculum Overview

We follow the CBSE curriculum with a focus on conceptual understanding and practical application.

🧒

Pre-Primary (Nursery - UKG)

Play-based learning, activity-oriented approach, language development, motor skills, and social skills through fun activities.

📖

Primary (Class 1-5)

Foundation building in English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS, Computers, Art, Music, and Physical Education.

📘

Middle (Class 6-8)

Core subjects + Science, Social Studies, Computer Science, Third Language options, and life skills education.

🎓

Secondary (Class 9-10)

Board-focused curriculum with Science/Commerce streams preparation, project work, and competitive exam guidance.

🏅

Senior Secondary (11-12)

Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, and Humanities streams with career counseling and entrance exam support.
