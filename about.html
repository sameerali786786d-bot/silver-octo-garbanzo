Our Story

Established in 1998, Green Valley Public School has been a beacon of quality education for over 25 years. Founded with the vision of providing holistic education that balances academics with character development, the school has grown from a small institution to one of the most respected schools in the region.

Vision & Mission

🎯

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that nurtures innovative thinkers, compassionate leaders, and responsible global citizens who contribute positively to society.

🚀

Our Mission

To provide a stimulating learning environment that encourages curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking while instilling strong moral values and life skills.

💎

Core Values

Integrity, Excellence, Respect, Responsibility, Empathy, and Lifelong Learning form the foundation of everything we do at Green Valley.

Principal's Message

👩‍🏫

Mrs. Anjali Sharma

Principal, Green Valley Public School

"It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Green Valley Public School. Our school is not just a place of learning; it is a second home where every child is valued, encouraged, and empowered to reach their full potential. We believe education goes beyond textbooks — it is about building character, fostering creativity, and preparing students for the challenges of tomorrow. Together with our dedicated staff and supportive parents, we strive to create an environment where excellence is a habit and every student thrives."
